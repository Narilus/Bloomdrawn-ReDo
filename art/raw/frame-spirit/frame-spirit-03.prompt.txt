Painterly gothic fantasy ornament for a game card frame, fine linework and crisp edges, drawn as separate decorative pieces lit softly from the upper left. Each piece is a single solid object with wide empty space all around it, and no piece touches another. Bold, simple silhouettes that still read when shown very small, with fine detail inside them. Antique gold filigree accents. No card, no panel, no frame behind the pieces, no scenery, no faces, no figures, no text, no letters, no logos.

The ornament is made of shrine things: gold leaf over carved pale wood, plain ash-grey shrine cloth, blank paper talisman strips with no writing on them, red-and-white braided prayer cord, and small round brass bells. The corner is a gold-leaf shrine bracket with a small brass bell hanging from it on a braided cord and two blank paper talismans fluttering beside it. The crest is a small flame of pale gold fire inside a ring of gold leaf. The border strip is a braided prayer cord of red and white, alike along its length, with small blank paper tassels hanging from it at even spacing.

Colours: gold leaf #C9A45A, ash grey, smoke lavender, a little pale gold flame, with antique gold. No animals, no ghosts, no writing on the paper, no glow points.
