Painterly gothic fantasy ornament for a game card frame, fine linework and crisp edges, drawn as separate decorative pieces lit softly from the upper left. Each piece is a single solid object with wide empty space all around it, and no piece touches another. Bold, simple silhouettes that still read when shown very small, with fine detail inside them. Antique gold filigree accents. No card, no panel, no frame behind the pieces, no scenery, no faces, no figures, no text, no letters, no logos.

The ornament belongs to the flowering growth itself, not to any one person: soft petal-ivory and blush flowers whose petals are skin with fine veins, a few small closed eyes set in the hearts of flowers, drifts of pale gold pollen, and pale fleshy stems. The corner is a cluster of three blush skin-petal flowers on a curving pale stem, the largest with one small closed eye at its heart. The crest is a single round open flower of ivory skin petals with one small open eye of soft hazel at its centre. The border strip is a pale fleshy stem running evenly along its length with small blush buds and leaves at even spacing and a light dusting of pollen.

Colours: petal ivory, blush #F2C4C4, flesh pink #E8A0A8, raw pink, a little antique gold. No gore, no rot, no faces other than the small eyes.


Three pieces, laid out apart on the canvas:

Top left: one corner piece shaped like a right angle, an L. Its outer corner sits near the top-left of the canvas; one arm runs right along an imagined top edge and the other runs down along an imagined left edge, each arm about a third of the canvas long, thick at the corner and tapering to a fine point. The main motif sits on the corner itself.

Top right: one crest, a small emblem symmetrical left to right, about a fifth of the canvas wide.

Bottom: one long, narrow horizontal border strip running across nearly the whole width of the canvas and about a tenth of its height tall, alike along its whole length and symmetrical end to end.
