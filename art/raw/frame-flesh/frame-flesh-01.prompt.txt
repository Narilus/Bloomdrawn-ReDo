Painterly gothic fantasy ornament for a game card frame, fine linework and crisp edges, drawn as separate decorative pieces lit softly from the upper left. Each piece is a single solid object with wide empty space all around it, and no piece touches another. Bold, simple silhouettes that still read when shown very small, with fine detail inside them. Antique gold filigree accents. No card, no panel, no frame behind the pieces, no scenery, no faces, no figures, no text, no letters, no logos.

The ornament is grown from living flesh and surgery: rose-pink and blush skin petals with fine darker veins, stitched onto antique gold filigree with neat black surgical thread; small closed embryo buds and ivory seed pods; thin twisting umbilical vines of raw pink with visible muscle striation. The corner is one opening blush flower with a closed embryo bud at its heart, its petals sewn to the gold. The crest is a small seed pod split open into a rose-pink bloom, held in a gold setting. The border strip is one umbilical vine winding evenly along a thin gold rod, with small stitched petals at even spacing.

Colours: rose #C9566A, blush, raw pink, ivory and a little arterial red, with antique gold. No gore without the flower, no rot, no machine parts.


Three pieces, laid out apart on the canvas:

Its outer corner sits near the top-left of the canvas; one arm runs right along an imagined top edge and the other runs down along an imagined left edge, each arm about a third of the canvas long, thick at the corner and tapering to a fine point.
