Facing left: the whole figure turned toward the left edge of the image. A towering shrine oracle twice the height of a person, a tall serene woman with the features of a kirin: a single long branching horn of gold-leafed bone rising from her brow, small patches of pale gold scales on her cheeks and forearms, a long flowing smoke-lavender mane down her back, and slender deer-like legs ending in black hooves beneath her robe. Layered ash-grey and white shrine robes with long hanging sleeves and a gold-leaf collar. Behind her hang hundreds of paper talismans on red cords in a great fan, each with a small bronze bell. A ring of pale gold flames burns in the air around her shoulders like a halo. One five-fingered hand holds a tall shrine staff hung with prayer ribbons and bells; the other is raised, two fingers extended. A single blush flower of skin has rooted at the base of her horn, fine pink roots spreading into her hair. The most beautiful part is the halo of pale gold flames. Ash grey, gold leaf, smoke lavender, pale gold and blush palette.
