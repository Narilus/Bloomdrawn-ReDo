Agathe's censer: a green-gold brass censer hanging from a short chain, its domed lid pierced with rings of small circles, a thin ribbon of pale smoke rising from it; a bruise-violet cloth tassel tied to the chain; white chalk dust caked on its base; one small flesh-pink tentacle tip curling out of a vent in the lid. Bruise violet, green-gold and candle-wax ivory palette.
