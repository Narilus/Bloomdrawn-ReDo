The ornament is made of cult-ritual things: carved dark wood and antique gold, bruise-violet velvet stole cloth with fringe, dripping candle-wax ivory, and thin flesh-coloured tentacles with visible muscle striation curling out from under the cloth. The corner is a short lit candle of ivory wax set in a gold holder, wax running down, with a violet stole draped across both arms of the corner and a tentacle curling from beneath it. The crest is a small gold censer hanging on a chain, a thin curl of pale smoke above it. The border strip is a violet stole with a gold embroidered edge and short fringe, alike along its length, with a tentacle wound loosely around it.

Colours: bruise violet #7A4FA0, sickly green-gold #A8A04A, candle-wax ivory, with antique gold. Nothing from the sea: no fins, scales or shells.
