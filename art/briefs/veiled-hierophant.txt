Facing left: the whole figure turned toward the left edge of the image. A towering cult high priestess twice the height of a person, standing on a round stone dais drawn with white chalk circles. Heavy layered bruise-violet vestments from throat to floor, a long stole embroidered in green-gold thread, and a tall pointed mitre draped with a veil of candle-wax ivory lace that falls over her eyes; below the veil her face is tipped upward, rapt and serene, lips parted in a smile of devotion. Lit candles stand on both shoulders, their wax dripping down the robe. Her arms are raised wide in benediction: one five-fingered hand swings a great brass censer on a long chain trailing pale smoke; the other holds up a carved bone idol of a closed flower. From her wide sleeves, from the back of her vestments and from the chalk circles beneath the hem rise many thick flesh-pink tentacles with visible muscle striation, coiling upward around her like a choir in procession, their tips opening into blush flowers of skin. The most beautiful part is the great crown of tentacles opening into flowers behind her raised arms. Bruise violet, sickly green-gold, candle-wax ivory and blush palette.
