Facing left: the whole figure turned toward the left edge of the image. A towering cult high priest twice the height of a person, standing on a round stone dais drawn with white chalk circles. Heavy layered bruise-violet vestments, a long stole embroidered in green-gold thread, and a tall pointed mitre draped with a veil of candle-wax ivory lace that hides the face down to a thin smiling mouth. Lit candles stand on both shoulders, their wax dripping down the robe. One five-fingered hand swings a great brass censer on a long chain trailing pale smoke; the other holds up a carved bone idol of a closed flower. From the wide sleeves and from the chalk circles beneath the hem rise eight thick flesh-pink tentacles with visible muscle striation, coiling upward around the figure, their tips opening into blush flowers of skin. The most beautiful part is the ring of tentacles opening into flowers. Bruise violet, sickly green-gold, candle-wax ivory and blush palette.
