Facing left: the whole figure turned toward the left edge of the image. A tall paper talisman as long as a person hanging from the crossbar of a small black lacquered shrine frame planted in a round stone base. The paper is ash grey, creased and edged in gold leaf, painted with a single closed blush flower instead of any writing. Red cords tied with three small bronze bells run down its length, and a prayer ribbon of smoke lavender hangs beside it. A small pale gold flame burns at its lower tip without burning the paper, and fine pink roots have grown into the paper from the flower. The most beautiful part is the pale gold flame at its tip. Ash grey, gold leaf, red, smoke lavender, pale gold and blush palette.
