The ornament is made from a well-off traveller's things: worn oxblood leather with neat cream saddle stitching, dark brass fittings with rivets, small buckles, and the gears and engraved case of a pocket watch. The corner is a dark brass corner cap riveted over oxblood leather, engraved with fine scrollwork, with a small closed flower bud worked into its clasp. The crest is a small closed brass pocket-watch case on a short chain. The border strip is a narrow band of oxblood leather with cream stitching down both edges and small brass rivets at even spacing, alike along its length.

Colours: oxblood #8A4038, dark brass, cream, a little antique gold. No stars, no planets, no clock faces with numbers, no glass reflections.
