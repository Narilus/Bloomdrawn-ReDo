A great chalk sigil circle on dark stone with a ring of guttering ivory candles, and from its centre a tall surging column of many flesh-pink tentacles of smooth skin bursting upward, a gold-embroidered violet stole caught and lifted on them.

Colours: bruise violet #7A4FA0, sickly green-gold #A8A04A, candle-wax ivory, flesh pink, with antique gold. Nothing from the sea: no fins, shells or suckers.
