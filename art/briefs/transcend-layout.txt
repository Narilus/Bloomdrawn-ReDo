One single piece only, centred on the canvas with wide empty space all around it: a raised crest that crowns the top edge of a card. It is symmetrical left to right, about two and a half times as wide as it is tall, and its bottom edge is one straight, flat horizontal line where it sits on the card's top edge; everything rises from that line into a peak at the centre. One bold, simple silhouette that still reads very small, with fine detail inside it.
