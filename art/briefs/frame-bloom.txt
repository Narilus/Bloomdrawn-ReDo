The ornament belongs to the flowering growth itself, not to any one person: soft petal-ivory and blush flowers whose petals are skin with fine veins, a few small closed eyes set in the hearts of flowers, drifts of pale gold pollen, and pale fleshy stems. The corner is a cluster of three blush skin-petal flowers on a curving pale stem, the largest with one small closed eye at its heart. The crest is a single round open flower of ivory skin petals with one small open eye of soft hazel at its centre. The border strip is a pale fleshy stem running evenly along its length with small blush buds and leaves at even spacing and a light dusting of pollen.

Colours: petal ivory, blush #F2C4C4, flesh pink #E8A0A8, raw pink, a little antique gold. No gore, no rot, no faces other than the small eyes.
