A crest of nine fox tails fanned upward in a half circle like an open hand fan, the tallest at the centre forming the peak: thick ash-grey and white fur with soft lavender shading, each tail tip burning with a small flame of pale gold fire. The tails rise from one gold-leaf knot at the base, tied with a red-and-white braided prayer cord whose ends hang to either side with blank paper talisman tassels and one small round brass bell at the centre.
