A heavy bruise-violet velvet stole embroidered with gold thread hangs taut in the air like a ward, its fringe stirring; behind it flesh-pink tentacles of smooth skin coil together into a living wall, and three short ivory candles burn in a row before it on a chalk circle.

Colours: bruise violet #7A4FA0, sickly green-gold #A8A04A, candle-wax ivory, flesh pink, with antique gold. Nothing from the sea: no fins, shells or suckers.
