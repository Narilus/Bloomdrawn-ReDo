A ritual sword of dark iron with a gold cross-guard, its point driven down into a chalk sigil circle on dark flagstones, the chalk lines cracking where it strikes; two thin flesh-pink tentacles of smooth skin wind in a spiral around the iron blade from the guard to the point. Short ivory candles burn at the edge of the circle and their smoke curls past the hilt.

Colours: dark iron, bruise violet #7A4FA0, sickly green-gold #A8A04A, candle-wax ivory, flesh pink, chalk white, with antique gold. Nothing from the sea: no fins, shells or suckers.
