Facing left: body and face turned toward the left edge of the image. A towering abbess half grown into a root-tree, twice the height of a person. Bruise-violet vestments with brass embroidery hang from her shoulders; below the waist her body splits into thick pale roots that grip the ground and spread wide. Her right hand holds a tall brass crozier wound with blush petals and small buds. Where her face should be is a smooth dark hollow, framed by a ring of ivory lilies and a tall ivory wimple. Thin pale roots climb her sleeves. Violet, brass and ivory palette, still and solemn.
