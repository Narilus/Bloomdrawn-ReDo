A small gothic altar crest with three lit candles of dripping ivory wax standing in a row, the tallest at the centre forming the peak and a shorter one to each side, each with a small warm flame. Two flesh-pink tentacles of smooth, soft skin with paler undersides weave between all three candles in loose open loops, passing in front of one candle and behind the next, so the three candles and the tentacles read as one interlaced knot spread wide across the crest; the tentacle tips curl outward at the two low ends. The base is a low pointed arch of carved dark wood and antique gold with a bruise-violet velvet stole draped across it, gold embroidery and fringe hanging at its ends. The tentacles are bold and easy to see.

Colours: bruise violet #7A4FA0, sickly green-gold #A8A04A, candle-wax ivory, flesh pink, with antique gold. Nothing from the sea: no fins, shells or suckers.
