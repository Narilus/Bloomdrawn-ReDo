A small gothic altar crest built around one tall lit candle: a single tall candle of dripping ivory wax stands at the centre and forms the peak, with a small warm flame on top. Two thick flesh-pink tentacles of smooth, soft, wet-looking skin like a tongue, with faint lengthwise muscle lines and paler undersides rise from either end of the base and coil in a tight spiral around the candle, wrapping it two or three times and meeting just below the flame, their tips curling outward. The base is a low pointed arch of carved dark wood and antique gold with a bruise-violet velvet stole draped across it, gold embroidery and fringe hanging at its ends. The tentacles are the boldest shapes in the crest.

Colours: bruise violet #7A4FA0, sickly green-gold #A8A04A, candle-wax ivory, flesh pink, with antique gold. Nothing from the sea: no fins, shells or suckers, and the skin is smooth with no scales or crosshatch pattern.
