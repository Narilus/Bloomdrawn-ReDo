# Weapons (M6.8)

Status: **draft for the owner, 14 Nov.** Built from the owner's guidelines (14 Nov), `m6-depth.md` ("Weapons") and DESIGN.md. Numbers are placeholders for the lab. Questions are at the end; nothing is built until they're answered.

## Rules

- **Anyone can wield any weapon,** one weapon per character. A weapon copy has one wielder at a time; equipping it on someone else takes it off the first, after a confirm that names her.
- **Every weapon has a main stat and an effect.** The main stat always applies. The effect applies wherever its words make sense: a signature effect that names its owner's card or ultimate does nothing on anyone else, and nothing says so but its text.
- **Rarities:** SSR (the eight signatures), SR and R.
- **Grafts.** A spare copy of the same weapon grafts into it, from +0 up to +5. Each graft raises the **effect** by a tenth of its +0 value, so +5 is ×1.5. The main stat never changes. A +0 weapon does its whole job; grafts are for players who love a weapon.
- **Salvage.** An unequipped, unlocked copy can be salvaged for Pollen (see "Salvage" below). A **lock** on a copy keeps it from salvage, both by hand and automatic.
- **No weapon levels** (my recommendation, question 1). The main stat is a share of the wielder's own stat, so it grows as she levels and is never left behind.

### Main stats (placeholders)

A share of the wielder's stat, after levels and ascensions.

| Rarity | Attack, Defense or HP | Crit Rate | Crit Damage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SSR | +30% | +12% | +24% |
| SR | +18% | — | — |
| R | +10% | — | — |

For comparison, an ascension is about +10% of a stat's level-40 growth. So an SSR is worth about three ascensions on one stat, an SR about two, and an R about one.

## Pulls

- **The weapon banner** works like the character banner, on Purified Blooms, with its own pity:
  - a Focus signature, chosen and changed freely;
  - the same 50/50;
  - pity at 30;
  - a ten-pull's SR.

  An SSR is a signature, and its pool is all eight, the starters' included (question 4).
- **SR and R results on both banners** (`m6-depth.md`):
  - an SR is a random SR weapon;
  - an R is half the time a random R weapon, and half the time today's Memories and Ducats.
- A result reads as before, plus what happened to the copy: "Surgeon's Scalpel (new)", "(grafted to +2)" or "(salvaged: 10 Pollen)".

### The two tickboxes

These go on both banner screens, since SR and R weapons come from both. They're one setting, saved in the profile, and both are off by default.

- ☐ **Graft new copies automatically.** A copy of a weapon you already have grafts into your highest-graft copy below +5, preferring one that's equipped.
- ☐ **Salvage new copies of weapons already at +5.** A copy that isn't grafted becomes Pollen if you hold that weapon at +5.

They don't conflict. Grafting goes first, and salvage only takes what grafting left. With both on, copies grow your weapons up to +5 and turn into Pollen after that. With salvage alone, unmaxed copies are kept as spares. A weapon's first copy is never salvaged, and neither is any copy of a locked weapon.

### Salvage

**Recommendation: salvage gives Pollen,** and the Profile Shop (M6.9) sells weapon copies for Pollen alongside character dupes. That gives salvage a purpose: copies you can't use become the weapon you want.
- Pollen is already the currency that spare copies turn into.
- DESIGN.md has the shop selling "dupes, weapons" for it.
- A second salvage currency would add to the hoard and do the same job.

| Salvaged | Pollen | Shop price (M6.9, proposed) |
|---|---:|---:|
| R copy | 2 | 30 |
| SR copy | 10 | 60 |
| SSR copy | 40 (as a character past C5) | 150, for a chosen signature |

Salvaging a grafted copy pays for every copy grafted into it: a +2 SR pays 30.

## Dupe names

- **C stands for Corolla:** the ring of petals of a flower. Each copy of a character opens one petal of her Corolla, C1 to C5.
  - The C stays, and the Bloom gets the name.
  - The tab and the banner say "Corolla"; tooltips say "C3 · Corolla 3 of 5".
- **Weapons keep +1 to +5, called Grafts:** "Graft 2 of 5". Grafting copies onto a weapon fits the Bloom's own verb (DESIGN: "The Bloom roots, grafts, buds and flowers"), and "+2" reads differently from "C2".

## How it's built (for reference)

- **Content:** `weapon({ id, name, rarity, domain?, signature?, main, effect })`. The effect is triggers and modifiers, like a passive. A signature's effect names its owner's card or ultimate by ID, as Corolla levels already do. The engine never asks who the signature is for, so "doesn't apply" falls out of the data.
- **Engine:**
  - a member's weapon joins her stats (main stat) and her hooks (effect, held by her);
  - grafts scale the effect's amounts;
  - one new modifier filter, "only this card" or "only this ultimate", for the signatures.
- **Save (v11):**
  - weapon copies as `{ uid, weapon, graft, locked? }`;
  - `equipped` as character → copy;
  - the two tickboxes.
- **Art:** an icon for each signature weapon through `npm run art`, briefs from her look. SR and R weapons use a drawn glyph per kind (blade, censer, lamp, bell) until the art pass.
